## Inclusion rule

An economy enters if its Maddison GDP per capita and population are complete for every year 1950–2022, with no interpolation and no splices (open issue 17; S30 confirmed by Sid on 11 October 2026).

- **United Arab Emirates fails**: Maddison GDP per capita is missing in 1991, 1992. Excluded; no S31 run.
