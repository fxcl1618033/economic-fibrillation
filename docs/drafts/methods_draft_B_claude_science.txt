4. Crisis dating
Crisis years are taken exogenously from the Kose–Terrones definition of a global recession, which identifies contractions in world real GDP per capita accompanied by broad-based declines in other measures of global activity. This yields five dates in our window: 1975, 1982, 1991, 2009 and 2020. These dates are fixed before any statistic is computed and are never adjusted, re-weighted, or selected on the basis of the synchronization series. We use only the crisis onset years; no crisis is assigned a duration.

The count matters for interpretation and is stated plainly: five events in 53 years. Every crisis-conditioned test in this paper therefore rests on five degrees of freedom at most, and the leave-one-out analysis of §7.3 shows that the effective count for the pre-crisis result is smaller still.

6.7 Critical slowing down. We test the standard early-warning battery — rising lag-1 autocorrelation, rising variance, and rising restoring-time — on the pre-crisis windows. All three are reported as null in Results. Annual data over 8-year pre-crisis windows gives these estimators very few effective degrees of freedom, so the null is weak evidence in the statistical sense and we do not read it as establishing an absence.

It is not, however, an empty result. Rising autocorrelation and variance are the specific signature of a system approaching a bifurcation — the slow eigenvalue going to zero as a fixed point loses stability. In the Ashwin–Wieczorek–Vitolo–Cox (2012) taxonomy this is B-tipping, and it is only one of three routes; noise-induced (N-) and rate-induced (R-) tipping produce no such precursor, because in neither case does the system's restoring rate vanish before the transition. The absence of the slowing-down fingerprint is therefore consistent with a non-bifurcation transition, and is informative about which class of tipping is in play even where it is uninformative about proximity to a threshold. We record the classification here and leave the mechanism argument to the Discussion; nothing in this paper identifies which non-bifurcation route applies.
