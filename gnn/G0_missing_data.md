| `series_absent` | missing | the lender reports no series for this borrower |
| `series_gap` | missing | the lender's series for this borrower has no year-end value |
| `zero` | true zero | a reported value of exactly zero |
| `no_denominator` | missing | the flow is observed but its denominator is missing (the receiving economy's GDP, or the sender's total) |
| `other_report_only` | missing | this side's report of the flow is missing; the other side's report exists and is not used to fill it |

**Plain answer to "true zero or missing":** every node-feature gap is missing data (no node series has a structural zero). For trade, the IMF file records no zeros at all (0 of 138,217 rows), so no trade gap can be confirmed as a true zero: pairs with no recorded flow while both sides report other partners (`unrecorded`) are probable zeros, mostly South Africa's trade with Asian and Middle Eastern economies during the apartheid sanctions (1970–91) and China–South Korea before 1990, but they are masked like missing data. For bank claims no reported value is exactly zero; every gap is missing data. For oil, a recorded production of zero is a true zero (observed), and South Korea, Switzerland, Belgium, Finland and Portugal are classed as true-zero producers in every year (Sid, 13 October 2026; reason in section 4); every remaining oil gap is missing data.
