# Source note

The graph dataset itself (yearly trade, bank-lending and oil edges) is built on the user's machine into `build/gnn/g0`
and is not in this repository. The files here list coverage and gaps only. Bank-lending edges use the Bank for
International Settlements' Locational Banking Statistics (data.bis.org; source: BIS), under the BIS terms of use for
statistics (https://www.bis.org/terms_statistics.htm); the BIS does not endorse this work. Trade edges use the IMF's
IMTS under the IMF's terms (https://www.imf.org/en/About/copyright-and-terms). `wb_vintage_comparison.csv.gz` holds
World Bank GDP (CC BY 4.0). See `DATA_LICENSES.md` at the top of the repository.
